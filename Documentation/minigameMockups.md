# Initial Minigame Mockups 

---

## Governing Philosophy
- Adding context to abstract concepts and allowing students to 'play' with those concepts will enrich the learning outcomes of those students

### Overall Target MATH 1010U Learning Outcomes:
- Recognize the appropriate technique to solve a problem
- Justify the conclusion to a mathematical problem 
- Demonstrate an understanding of formal definitions of limits, derivatives, and integrals

---

## Minigame 1: Limits 

### Target MATH 1010U Learning Objectives:
- Evaluate limits graphically, numerically, and algebraically

### Target MATH 1010U Topics:
- Limit laws 
- limits at infinity 
- L'Hopital's Rule (illustrated)

### Minigame Idea:
1) Adjust the limit: 
    - Players will be given an equation and a specific target boundary (ex. baking a cake, max temp of 450 C) 
    - The system will be initial set up to miss that target or boundary, either via the wrong limit value or via the given equation
    - Players can adjust the limit value or equation, and then simulate how taking the new limit will effect the system 

### Tutorial Idea
- An interactive lesson on what limits are, allowing players to 'scrub' approaching a limit like a playback bar on a youtube video

---

## Minigame 2: Derivatives

### Target MATH 1010U Learning Objectives:
- Apply the rules of differentiation 
- Apply the interpretations of derivatives to a variety of application problems

### Target MATH 1010U Topics: 
- Derivatives of functions
- Rates of change in the natural and social sciences
- Related rates 
- Max values

### Minigame Idea:
1) Derivi-Bird:
    - Players will be given a 'bird' and an obstacle course
    - Players will then adjust the derivative equation that represents the birds acceleration due to gravity to enable the bird to clear the course
2) Wildlife Management:
    - Players will be given a 'predator' population and a 'prey' population (ex. wolves and rabbits)
    - Players will need to adjust the derivative equations to ensure the populations remain in balance (or see how the populations can collapse)

### Tutorial Idea: 
- Interactive tutorial that demonstrates how derivatives rise from the 'average value' equation by taking the limit of the delta between values to zero

## Minigame 3: Integrals 

### Target MATH 1010U Learning Outcomes:
- Preform the basic calculations related to integration 
- Apply the interpretations of integrals to a variety of application problems

### Target MATH 1010U Topics:
- Optimization Problems 
- Antiderivatives 
- Areas and distances 
- The definite integral 
- Areas between Curves

### Minigame Ideas:
1) Integral Tetris: 
    - Like Tetris, with the twist that players will need to take a function and preform an integral over a specific interval to make the next shape that will drop down 
    - Difficulty levels can change what percentage of a row needs to be filled in in order to 'clear' a row
2) Generate Shapes: 
    - Players take a function and preform an integral over a specific interval to make 'puzzle pieces' to fill in the given outline of a larger shape 
    - Difficulty levels can change the percentage of the shape that needs to be filled in, and punish the percentage of the pieces 'outside' the shape
3) Calculate Totals:
    - Players will be given a target value for an integral that represents a system, (ex. a bunny population or distance traveled)
    - Players will then find and take the integration of an appropriate function to achieve that total 
    - Difficulty can adjust the 'score' given based on the percent error of the achieved total to the target total

### Tutorial Idea: 
- An interactive tutorial that allows players to interact with how taking the limit to zero of the formal definition of an integral arrives at the integration used in game 
- Interactive visual representation of the 'leftside rightside' boxes used to approximate integration
